Privacy

MacroScope is a university project (INFS3208, The University of Queensland). This page says what it keeps about you and why.

What we keep

  • Your login: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google. Logins are handled by Google Cloud Identity Platform; MacroScope never sees your password.
  • Your details: what you enter on "My details" (sex, date of birth, height, weight, activity, goal, diet, and pregnancy or breastfeeding if you choose to say).
  • Your food: the meals and recipes you log, and the daily nutrient totals worked out from them.

Why

Only to work out your targets, show how your eating compares with them, and suggest foods. Nothing is used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone.

Where it's kept

In databases on Google Cloud in Sydney, Australia, that only MacroScope's own services can reach. The website keeps your login in your browser so you stay logged in, and nothing else.

Deleting it

You can delete any meal or recipe yourself in the app. To have your account and everything in it deleted, email christokachappilly@gmail.com.

Not medical advice

MacroScope gives estimates from food composition data. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about your diet, especially in pregnancy.
